# Startup Launch & Growth Engineering Playbook

Systematic frameworks for validating ideas, achieving Product-Market Fit, and engineering high-conversion viral loops.

## Core Growth Mechanics:
1. **Sean Ellis 40% PMF Test**: Survey users: *"How would you feel if you could no longer use this product?"* Target > 40% "Very disappointed".
2. **Activation Funnel (Time-to-Value)**: Eliminate all onboarding friction so users experience the core "Aha! Moment" within the first 60 seconds of sign-up.
3. **K-Factor Viral Coefficient**: $K = i \times c$ (where $i$ = invites per user, $c$ = conversion rate per invite). If $K > 1$, growth is exponential.
